    WELCOME TO LINKWISP

    Connect your link service

    LinkWisp keeps history on this device. It needs your Worker only to create and resolve shareable short links.

    1. Enter the Worker address shown by local development or provided by the service owner.
    2. Enter its private access code. The code stays in this browser profile.
    3. LinkWisp will verify both without creating a link.
